FLASH YOUR DETECTOR

Your browser can't do this. Web Serial only works in Chrome, Edge, or another Chromium browser, on a computer (not a phone). This page needs to be loaded over HTTPS to flash a device.
  1. Plug your SquachWatch-CYD into this computer over USB.
  2. Click Connect & Install above.
  3. Pick the right serial port from the browser's popup.
  4. Click Install and leave it be — don't unplug until it says done.

Works in Chrome / Edge / Brave on desktop. No app, no drivers, nothing to install on your end — just the browser.
